Our Story

Rajasthali Handicrafts brings the textile heritage of Rajasthan to your wardrobe. Every saree is made by skilled artisan families using traditional techniques — block printing, bandhani tie-dye, Kota Doria weaving and zari work — passed down through generations.

Buying from us supports fair-wage craft livelihoods. We begin with sarees and will soon expand to kurtas and more.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do sarees come with a blouse piece?

Yes, most sarees include an unstitched blouse piece. Check the product description.

How long does delivery take?

3–7 business days across India.

What is your return policy?

Returns accepted within 7 days of delivery, unused with tags intact.

How should I care for my saree?

Dry clean for silk and zari work. Gentle cold hand wash for cotton and block prints. Do not wring or bleach.

Do you offer Cash on Delivery?

Yes, COD is available on most PIN codes.
